## Known limitations

DriveGuard Lab v1.0 supports only a deterministic one-dimensional longitudinal
lead-braking scenario and point-reference collision rule. Warning does not alter
motion; baseline AEB is a simple risk-to-action mapping. Event times are discrete
retained-frame observations. The project has no ACC, steering, lanes, perception,
noise, persistence, export, streaming, database, CARLA, OpenSCENARIO, real-vehicle
integration, safety certification, authentication, distributed rate limiting, or
enterprise deployment hardening.

Optional SUMO experiments remain adapter-local and do not change the API,
Dashboard, internal runner, schema, or safety claims.
